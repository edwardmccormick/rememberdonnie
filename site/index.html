In loving memory of

Donnie Edward McNeal Jr.

“DJ”

January 17, 1981 –to October 13, 2026

Great Engineer. Better person.

Donnie Edward McNeal Jr. - DJ to his family, Donnie to those of us who worked alongside him - was born on January 17, 1981, and left us on October 13, 2026. He was 45.

He was a great engineer - and, as anyone who worked alongside him will tell you, an even better person. He was an easy man to know, and a hard man to know well. A brilliant engineer who loved simple, elegant solutions. A hilarious guy who was surprisingly hard to make laugh.

We will miss him terribly.

This is a place to hold him up - a place to pay homage and share stories so all the people who loved him can find them, and each other.
